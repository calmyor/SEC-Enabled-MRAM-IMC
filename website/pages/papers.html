One measured macro, two levels of explanation.

The ESSCIRC paper introduces the SNR-boosting macro and measured headline results. The JSSC extension deepens the circuit, behavioral-model, fixed-point SEC, compute-SNDR methodology, and network-level evaluation.

Saion K. Roy, Han-Mo Ou, Mostafa G. Ahmed, Peter Deaville, Bonan Zhang, Naveen Verma, Pavan K. Hanumolu, and Naresh R. Shanbhag.

What the journal adds

From headline SNR to a reproducible compute-SNDR argument.

MODEL

Parasitic error structure

Location-dependent attenuation, behavioral/circuit correlation, and the mathematical basis for shared SEC factors.

CIRCUIT

OCCS operation

Auto-zero/evaluate behavior, level shifting, mismatch range, PVT sensitivity, and simulated area trade-offs.

METHOD

Compute SNDR

Per-column affine MMSE calibration, code-conditioned state sampling, probability weighting, and repeated captures.

SYSTEM

Bounded inference test

A measured final-layer ResNet-20 mapping with explicit digital, SEC-off, and SEC-on accuracy.

Reading guide

Match each claim to its evidence class.

Measured siliconDie/core dimensions, ADC-column SNDR, SEC improvement, energy/SNDR points, area/energy overhead, and last-layer accuracy.
Circuit simulationOCCS Monte Carlo bitline-voltage variation and the sensor area comparison.
Behavioral simulationParasitic transfer behavior, quantized SEC convergence, and precision-selection evidence.
ProjectionReducing SEC area from 12.2% to 3.7% by replacing register storage with SRAM.
Author claimThe “first” characterization language is attributed to the authors unless independently established.
